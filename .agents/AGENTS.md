# Project Rules

- **Blog Post Titles**: Always use sentence case for blog post titles (e.g., `"Smartphone power efficiency comparison"`, `"HDR headroom and compositor behavior"`), avoiding capitalizing every word (Title Case).
- **Execution & Reflection**: Clarify intent before taking complex actions. At the end of every turn, reflect explicitly on whether the user's exact instructions were fulfilled completely without adding unprompted work or missing key steps.
