This post records video format support tested in the iOS Photos app.

## Test setup

- **Device**: iPhone 14 Pro Max
- **Operating system**: iOS 27.0
- **App**: Photos

## Supported video formats

The Photos app supports **4K, 10-bit, 4:2:2 HDR video with PQ and BT.2020** on the iPhone 14 Pro Max running iOS 27.0.

| Video property | Tested format |
| :--- | :--- |
| Resolution | 4K |
| Bit depth | 10-bit |
| Chroma subsampling | 4:2:2 |
| Dynamic range | HDR |
| Transfer function | PQ |
| Color primaries | BT.2020 |
